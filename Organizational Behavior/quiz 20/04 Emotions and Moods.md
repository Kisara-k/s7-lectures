## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following statements accurately describe the relationship between emotions and feelings?  
A) ✗ Feelings occur after emotions, not before.  
B) ✗ Feelings include emotional sensations, not just physical; emotions are not purely psychological.  
C) ✗ Emotions and feelings are related but distinct concepts, not identical.  
D) ✓ Emotions are intense chemical responses lasting about six seconds; feelings are more cognitively processed and last longer.  

**Correct:** D


#### 2. Which factors primarily influence moods, distinguishing them from emotions?  
A) ✗ Moods are less intense and longer-lasting than emotions, not the reverse.  
B) ✓ Moods can develop without a clear stimulus, unlike emotions.  
C) ✓ Moods are influenced by environment, physiology, and mental state, and last longer than emotions.  
D) ✗ Emotions require a specific trigger; moods do not necessarily require one.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 3. Positive affect as a mood dimension includes which of the following states?  
A) ✗ Nervousness and anxiety are part of negative affect.  
B) ✓ Elation and joy are positive affect states.  
C) ✓ Boredom and fatigue represent low positive affect (lack of positive affect).  
D) ✓ Excitement and enthusiasm are positive affect states.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 4. According to research, how do negative moods affect cognitive processing compared to positive moods?  
A) ✗ Positive moods do not enhance deception detection better than negative moods.  
B) ✗ Negative moods do not always lead to irrational behavior.  
C) ✗ Negative moods do not necessarily impair rational thinking; they can enhance certain cognitive tasks.  
D) ✓ Negative moods improve recognition of truthful information.  

**Correct:** D


#### 5. Which of the following best explains the concept of emotional labour?  
A) ✗ Emotional labour involves displayed emotions, not just natural feelings.  
B) ✓ It is the expression of emotions required by the organization, which may differ from felt emotions.  
C) ✓ It involves modifying true feelings to meet organizational display rules.  
D) ✗ Emotional labour is not about suppressing all emotions, but managing displayed emotions.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 6. What is emotional dissonance?  
A) ✗ Emotional dissonance is not about inability to feel emotions.  
B) ✗ Emotional dissonance is when displayed and felt emotions differ, not when they match.  
C) ✓ It is the conflict between felt emotions and displayed emotions required by the organization.  
D) ✗ Deep acting tries to align felt emotions with display rules, not emotional dissonance itself.  

**Correct:** C


#### 7. Which of the following are components of Affective Events Theory (AET)?  
A) ✗ Emotions do impact organizational citizenship behavior.  
B) ✗ Emotional responses vary with mood and are not fixed.  
C) ✓ People low in emotional stability react more strongly to negative events.

**Correct:** C, D


#### 8. Which statements about personality’s influence on emotions and moods are true?

B) ✓ Personality traits influence frequency and intensity of emotions.
