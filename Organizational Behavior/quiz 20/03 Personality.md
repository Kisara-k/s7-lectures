## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following are considered hereditary determinants of personality?  
A) ✓ Physical stature and facial features are influenced by genetics.  
B) ✓ Temperament and biological rhythms have genetic bases.  
C) ✗ Personal values and beliefs are learned, not hereditary.  
D) ✗ Work experience and education are environmental factors.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 2. According to personality research, personality traits are considered more stable when:  
A) ✗ Immediate environmental factors cause variability, not stability.  
B) ✗ Personality changes more during adolescence, so rapid change means less stability.  
C) ✓ Traits occurring frequently across diverse situations indicate stability.  
D) ✗ Stability requires consistency across various situations, not just familiar ones.  

**Correct:** C


#### 3. Which of the following pairs correctly represent the Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI) dichotomies?  
A) ✓ Judging vs. Perceiving is part of MBTI.  
B) ✗ Thinking vs. Agreeableness mixes MBTI and Big Five traits incorrectly.  
C) ✓ Extraverted vs. Introverted is a core MBTI dimension.  
D) ✓ Sensing vs. Intuitive is an MBTI dichotomy.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 4. What is a major criticism of the MBTI as a personality assessment tool?  
A) ✗ MBTI measures 4 dichotomies, not just five traits.  
B) ✓ MBTI assumes personality is static and ignores development over time.  
C) ✗ MBTI focuses on traits, not values directly.  
D) ✗ MBTI is not based solely on genetics.  

**Correct:** B


#### 5. Which Big Five personality trait is most associated with a person’s ability to withstand stress?  
A) ✗ Openness relates to creativity, not stress tolerance.  
B) ✓ Emotional stability (low neuroticism) reflects stress resilience.  
C) ✗ Conscientiousness relates to reliability, not stress tolerance.  
D) ✗ Agreeableness relates to cooperation, not stress tolerance.  

**Correct:** B


#### 6. How does extraversion differ between introverts and extroverts in terms of environmental stimulation?  
A) ✓ Introverts seek quiet places to recover from sensory overload.  
B) ✓ Extroverts are energized by busy, social settings.  
C) ✗ Introverts avoid high stimulation environments.  
D) ✗ Extroverts are energized, not overwhelmed, by busy environments.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 7. Which of the following statements about agreeableness is true?  
A) ✓ Low agreeableness is linked to cold and disagreeable behavior.  
B) ✗ Agreeableness does not measure creativity.  
C) ✗ Highly agreeable people are cooperative, not antagonistic.  
D) ✗ Agreeableness is strongly related to cooperation.  

**Correct:** A


#### 8. Which Big Five trait is best described as a measure of reliability and organization?  
A) ✗ Openness relates to creativity and curiosity.  
B) ✗ Emotional stability relates to stress tolerance.  
C) ✓ Conscientiousness measures responsibility and organization.  
D) ✗ Extraversion relates to sociability.  

**Correct:** C


#### 9. Which of the following traits are part of the Dark Triad in personality research?  
A) ✓ Machiavellianism is one of the Dark Triad traits.  
B) ✓ Psychopathy is included in the Dark Triad.  
C) ✗ Agreeableness is a Big Five trait, not part of the Dark Triad.  
D) ✓ Narcissism is part of the Dark Triad.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 10. A person high in Machiavellianism is likely to:  
A) ✓ Maintain emotional distance and manipulate others to achieve goals.  
B) ✗ High Machs are emotionally detached, not empathetic.  
C) ✗ They often use others strategically, not avoid manipulation.  
D) ✗ They prioritize ends over ethical means.  

**Correct:** A


#### 11. Narcissism in organizational behavior is characterized by:  
A) ✓ Grandiose self-importance is a key feature.  
B) ✓ Arrogance and entitlement are common traits.  
C) ✓ Excessive need for admiration is typical.  
D) ✗ Narcissists can be adaptable in complex decisions.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 12. Psychopathy in the workplace is often associated with:  
A) ✓ Manipulativeness and lack of guilt are defining features.  
B) ✗ Psychopaths tend to be self-serving, not collaborative.  
C) ✗ Psychopaths lack empathy and remorse.  
D) ✗ They often engage in bullying, not avoid it.  

**Correct:** A


#### 13. Which type of bullying is motivated by enjoyment of tormenting others without a specific goal?  
A) ✗ Situational bullying is not a defined type here.  
B) ✓ Predatory bullying is bullying for enjoyment without purpose.  
C) ✗ Reactive bullying is a response, not enjoyment-driven.  
D) ✗ Instrumental bullying is goal-oriented.  

**Correct:** B


#### 14. According to Situation Strength theory, personality traits are more likely to predict behavior in:  
A) ✗ Strongly structured roles limit personality effects.  
B) ✓ Weak situations allow personality traits to influence behavior more.  
C) ✗ Strong situations suppress personality expression.  
D) ✗ Strong rules reduce personality’s predictive power.  

**Correct:** B


#### 15. Trait Activation Theory (TAT) suggests that:  
A) ✗ Personality traits remain relevant in organizational behavior.  
B) ✗ Situations do not suppress all personality differences.  
C) ✓ Certain situations activate specific traits more than others.  
D) ✗ Traits are not equally expressed in all situations.  

**Correct:** C


#### 16. Which of the following best describes terminal values?  
A) ✗ Instrumental values are modes of behavior, not terminal values.  
B) ✗ Values learned from culture are not specifically terminal values.  
C) ✓ Terminal values are desirable end states or life goals.  
D) ✗ Terminal values are relatively stable, not temporary.  

**Correct:** C


#### 17. Person-Job Fit theory argues that:  
A) ✓ Satisfaction is highest when personality and job type align.  
B) ✗ Personality impacts job satisfaction significantly.  
C) ✗ Opposite personality-job matches reduce satisfaction.  
D) ✗ Turnover is influenced by fit, not unrelated.  

**Correct:** A


#### 18. Person-Organization Fit theory emphasizes:  
A) ✗ Personality is important in recruitment decisions.  
B) ✓ Matching individual values with organizational culture improves outcomes.  
C) ✗ Employees often leave if values and culture mismatch.  
D) ✗ Hiring based solely on skills ignores cultural fit.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 19. How do values differ from personality in terms of origin?  
A) ✗ Values are learned, not genetically determined.  
B) ✗ Personality has a genetic basis, values are culturally transmitted.  
C) ✓ Personality is largely genetic; values are learned from environment.  
D) ✗ Both are not entirely hereditary.  

**Correct:** C


#### 20. Hofstede’s cultural dimensions framework is primarily used to:  
A) ✗ It does not directly evaluate job satisfaction.  
B) ✓ Analyze national differences in work-related values.  
C) ✗ It is unrelated to hereditary personality determinants.  
D) ✗ It does not measure individual personality traits.  

**Correct:** B
