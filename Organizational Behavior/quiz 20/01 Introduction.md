## Answers

#### 1. What are some of the key social functions that work serves?  
A) ✓ Provides social status in the community, a key social function of work  
B) ✓ Offers opportunities for self-actualization, part of work’s social and psychological functions  
C) ✗ Work does not guarantee financial independence without social interaction; this is inaccurate  
D) ✓ Serves as a means for identity and self-esteem, important social functions of work  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 2. Which of the following best describe the concept of alienation in the workplace?  
A) ✓ Feeling powerless and meaningless due to lack of work functions is central to alienation  
B) ✓ Experiencing normlessness when work lacks significance is part of alienation  
C) ✗ Feeling highly motivated and engaged is opposite to alienation  
D) ✗ Feeling a strong sense of belonging contradicts alienation  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 3. Organizational Behaviour (OB) primarily studies the impact of which three determinants on behaviour?  
A) ✓ Individuals, groups, and structure are the three determinants studied in OB  
B) ✗ Technology, finance, and marketing are not primary OB determinants  
C) ✗ Culture, politics, and economics are broader societal factors, not the core OB determinants  
D) ✗ Leadership style, motivation, and communication are topics but not the three main determinants  

**Correct:** A


#### 4. Which of the following are core topics studied in Organizational Behaviour?  
A) ✓ Motivation and leader behaviour are core OB topics  
B) ✓ Group structure and interpersonal communication are core OB topics  
C) ✗ Financial accounting and market analysis are not OB topics  
D) ✓ Conflict, negotiation, and change processes are core OB topics  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 5. Which disciplines contribute to the multidisciplinary nature of OB?  
A) ✓ Psychology and social psychology are key contributors  
B) ✓ Sociology and anthropology also contribute significantly  
C) ✓ Political science and neuroscience increasingly contribute to OB  
D) ✗ Chemistry and physics do not contribute to OB  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 6. How does sociology contribute to the study of Organizational Behaviour?  
A) ✓ By exploring how society shapes individual actions, sociology explains social context  
B) ✗ Measuring and explaining behaviour scientifically is psychology’s role  
C) ✗ Examining rites and rituals is anthropology’s focus  
D) ✗ Analyzing power within organizations is political science’s domain  

**Correct:** A


#### 7. What was a key finding of the original Hawthorne Studies?  
A) ✗ Money was not the primary cause of increased productivity according to original findings  
B) ✓ Employees performed better when managers treated them positively (human relations)  
C) ✗ Productivity did not decrease due to supervision in the original study  
D) ✗ Group pride was recognized later, not in the original findings  

**Correct:** B

#### 9. Why are absolutes difficult to establish in Organizational Behaviour?  
A) ✓ Human behaviour is complex and varies by situation, limiting absolutes

#### 10. What does the contingency approach in OB imply?
