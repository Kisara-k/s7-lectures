## 4 Emotions and Moods

## Key Points

#### 1. ⚡ Emotions and Feelings  
- Emotions are intense feelings triggered by specific events and involve chemical releases in the body lasting about six seconds.  
- It takes about 1/4 second for the brain to identify an emotional trigger and another 1/4 second to release emotion chemicals.  
- Feelings are the cognitive processing of emotions and last longer than emotions.  

#### 2. 🌥️ Moods and Affect  
- Moods are less intense than emotions, last from hours to days, and do not require a specific trigger.  
- Affect is a broad term that includes both emotions and moods.  

#### 3. ➕➖ Positive and Negative Affect  
- Positive affect includes emotions like joy, enthusiasm, and excitement; low positive affect includes boredom and fatigue.  
- Negative affect includes emotions like stress, anxiety, and nervousness; low negative affect includes calmness and serenity.  

#### 4. 🧠 Rationality and Emotions  
- Emotions are essential for rational thinking; brain injury studies show people need emotions to be rational.  
- Negative moods can improve the ability to recognize truthful information compared to happy moods.  

#### 5. ⚖️ Ethics and Emotions  
- Moral judgments are largely based on emotions such as sympathy, guilt, anger, and disgust rather than pure cognition.  

#### 6. 👤 Influences Based on Who You Are  
- Personality affects how frequently and intensely people experience emotions (affect intensity).  
- Older people experience fewer negative emotions and longer-lasting positive moods.  
- Women are generally more emotionally expressive and experience emotions more intensely than men, except for anger.  

#### 7. ⏰ Influences Based on Circumstances  
- Positive affect peaks in the evening and is lowest in the early morning; negative affect peaks overnight and is lowest later in the morning.  
- Positive affect is highest on Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays, and lowest on Mondays.  
- Weather has little effect on most people's moods.  
- Stressful work events increase negative moods and build up over time.  
- Physical and social activities increase positive moods more than sedentary or formal events.  
- Poor sleep quality reduces job satisfaction and increases irritability.  
- Exercise improves positive mood, especially in depressed individuals.  
- Low blood sugar is linked to irritability and combative behavior; high blood sugar is linked to depression.  

#### 8. 😌 Emotional Labour  
- Emotional labour is the effort to display organizationally desired emotions during work interactions.  
- Felt emotions are actual emotions; displayed emotions are those required by the organization.  
- Surface acting involves faking emotions; deep acting involves trying to change true feelings.  
- Emotional dissonance occurs when there is a conflict between felt and displayed emotions.  

#### 9. 📊 Affective Events Theory (AET)  
- Employees’ emotional reactions to workplace events influence job performance and satisfaction.  
- People low in emotional stability react more strongly to negative events.  
- Emotions affect organizational citizenship behavior, commitment, effort, intention to quit, and workplace deviance.  

#### 10. 🌟 Emotional Intelligence (EQ)  
- EQ is the ability to perceive, understand, and regulate one’s own and others’ emotions.  
- High EQ improves teamwork, communication, leadership, conflict resolution, and overall workplace performance.  
- EQ is considered a skill or competency, not a personality trait.  

#### 11. 🛠️ Personal Emotion Regulation Strategies  
- Mindfulness meditation, cognitive reappraisal, journaling, deep breathing, exercise, social support, setting boundaries, self-compassion, problem-solving, and professional help improve emotional regulation.  

#### 12. 🏢 Organizational Strategies to Improve EQ  
- Training programs, coaching, feedback, open communication, mindfulness practices, team-building, leadership development, diversity initiatives, conflict resolution training, and regular assessments enhance emotional intelligence in organizations.
