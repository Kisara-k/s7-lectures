## 2 Attitudes

## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following statements accurately describe the dispositional approach to attitude formation?  
A) Attitudes are primarily shaped by social context and interactions.  
B) Some individuals tend to be happy or unhappy on the job regardless of work conditions.  
C) Attitudes are stable predispositions similar to personality traits.  
D) Attitudes change significantly after a person changes jobs.  

#### 2. The three main components of an attitude include:  
A) Motivation, Personality, and Perception  
B) Cognition, Affect, and Behaviour  
C) Emotion, Intention, and Action  
D) Beliefs, Values, and Perception  

#### 3. Cognitive dissonance occurs when:  
A) Employees feel satisfied with their job outcomes exceeding expectations.  
B) People experience tension due to inconsistency between their actions and beliefs.  
C) There is incompatibility between two or more attitudes or between behaviour and attitudes.  
D) An individual’s behaviour is consistent with their attitudes.  

#### 4. Which of the following job dimensions is LEAST likely to directly influence job satisfaction?  
A) The employee’s ethnic origin and early family experiences  
B) The extent to which coworkers are friendly and supportive  
C) The availability of realistic promotional opportunities  
D) The technical and managerial abilities of supervisors  

#### 5. Organizational commitment is best characterized by:  
A) Strong belief in organizational goals and values  
B) Passive loyalty to the company without emotional attachment  
C) Desire to maintain membership in the organization  
D) Willingness to exert effort on behalf of the organization  

#### 6. Which of the following statements about perceived organizational support (POS) is TRUE?  
A) Employees with high POS perceptions tend to exhibit more citizenship behaviours.  
B) POS is unrelated to employee tardiness or customer service quality.  
C) POS depends solely on the fairness of pay and rewards.  
D) Supervisors’ supportiveness influences employees’ perceptions of organizational support.  

#### 7. Employee engagement differs from job satisfaction primarily because:  
A) Job satisfaction always leads to high levels of employee engagement.  
B) Disengaged employees are those who put in time but lack energy or attention.  
C) Engagement reflects a deep connection to the company beyond task performance.  
D) Engagement includes enthusiasm and passion for work, not just positive feelings.  

#### 8. Which of the following best explains why job attitudes such as job satisfaction, job involvement, and organizational commitment often overlap?  
A) Employees’ perceptions of pay and promotion opportunities affect only job satisfaction.  
B) Organizational citizenship behaviour is unrelated to these job attitudes.  
C) They measure completely different aspects of work experience with no relation.  
D) They are all influenced by the employee’s personality traits.

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following statements accurately describe the dispositional approach to attitude formation?  
A) ✗ Attitudes are primarily shaped by social context and interactions (this is social-information-processing approach).  
B) ✓ Some individuals tend to be happy or unhappy on the job regardless of work conditions.  
C) ✓ Attitudes are stable predispositions similar to personality traits.  
D) ✗ Attitudes change significantly after a person changes jobs (dispositional approach argues attitudes change little).  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 2. The three main components of an attitude include:  
A) ✗ Motivation, Personality, and Perception (not components of attitude).  
B) ✓ Cognition, Affect, and Behaviour (these are the three components described).  
C) ✗ Emotion, Intention, and Action (similar but not the exact components).  
D) ✗ Beliefs, Values, and Perception (related concepts but not attitude components).  

**Correct:** B


#### 3. Cognitive dissonance occurs when:  
A) ✗ Employees feel satisfied with their job outcomes exceeding expectations (this relates to satisfaction, not dissonance).  
B) ✓ People experience tension due to inconsistency between their actions and beliefs.  
C) ✓ There is incompatibility between two or more attitudes or between behaviour and attitudes.  
D) ✗ An individual’s behaviour is consistent with their attitudes (no dissonance if consistent).  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 4. Which of the following job dimensions is LEAST likely to directly influence job satisfaction?  
A) ✓ The employee’s ethnic origin and early family experiences (individual differences, not a job dimension).  
B) ✗ The extent to which coworkers are friendly and supportive (important dimension).  
C) ✗ The availability of realistic promotional opportunities (important dimension).  
D) ✗ The technical and managerial abilities of supervisors (a key dimension).  

**Correct:** A


#### 5. Organizational commitment is best characterized by:  
A) ✓ Strong belief in organizational goals and values.  
B) ✗ Passive loyalty to the company without emotional attachment (commitment goes beyond passive loyalty).  
C) ✓ Desire to maintain membership in the organization.  
D) ✓ Willingness to exert effort on behalf of the organization.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 6. Which of the following statements about perceived organizational support (POS) is TRUE?  
A) ✓ Employees with high POS perceptions tend to exhibit more citizenship behaviours.  
B) ✗ POS is unrelated to employee tardiness or customer service quality (POS is linked to lower tardiness and better service).  
C) ✗ POS depends solely on the fairness of pay and rewards (also influenced by voice in decisions and supervisor support).  
D) ✓ Supervisors’ supportiveness influences employees’ perceptions of organizational support.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 7. Employee engagement differs from job satisfaction primarily because:  
A) ✗ Job satisfaction always leads to high levels of employee engagement (not necessarily true; they are related but distinct).  
B) ✓ Disengaged employees are those who put in time but lack energy or attention.  
C) ✓ Engagement reflects a deep connection to the company beyond task performance.  
D) ✓ Engagement includes enthusiasm and passion for work, not just positive feelings.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 8. Which of the following best explains why job attitudes such as job satisfaction, job involvement, and organizational commitment often overlap?  
A) ✗ Employees’ perceptions of pay and promotion opportunities affect only job satisfaction (these can influence multiple attitudes).  
B) ✗ Organizational citizenship behaviour is unrelated to these job attitudes (it is related and influenced by attitudes).  
C) ✗ They measure completely different aspects of work experience with no relation (they overlap significantly).  
D) ✓ They are all influenced by the employee’s personality traits.  

**Correct:** D
