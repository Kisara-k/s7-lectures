## 2 Attitudes

## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following statements accurately describe the dispositional approach to attitude formation?  
A) Attitudes are stable predispositions similar to personality traits.  
B) Attitudes change significantly after a person changes jobs.  
C) Some individuals tend to be happy or unhappy on the job regardless of work conditions.  
D) Attitudes are primarily shaped by social context and interactions.  

#### 2. The three main components of an attitude include:  
A) Cognition, Affect, and Behaviour  
B) Beliefs, Values, and Perception  
C) Emotion, Intention, and Action  
D) Motivation, Personality, and Perception  

#### 3. Cognitive dissonance occurs when:  
A) An individual’s behaviour is consistent with their attitudes.  
B) There is incompatibility between two or more attitudes or between behaviour and attitudes.  
C) Employees feel satisfied with their job outcomes exceeding expectations.  
D) People experience tension due to inconsistency between their actions and beliefs.  

#### 4. Which of the following job dimensions is LEAST likely to directly influence job satisfaction?  
A) The technical and managerial abilities of supervisors  
B) The availability of realistic promotional opportunities  
C) The employee’s ethnic origin and early family experiences  
D) The extent to which coworkers are friendly and supportive  

#### 5. Organizational commitment is best characterized by:  
A) Passive loyalty to the company without emotional attachment  
B) Strong belief in organizational goals and values  
C) Willingness to exert effort on behalf of the organization  
D) Desire to maintain membership in the organization  

#### 6. Which of the following statements about perceived organizational support (POS) is TRUE?  
A) POS is unrelated to employee tardiness or customer service quality.  
B) Employees with high POS perceptions tend to exhibit more citizenship behaviours.  
C) POS depends solely on the fairness of pay and rewards.  
D) Supervisors’ supportiveness influences employees’ perceptions of organizational support.  

#### 7. Employee engagement differs from job satisfaction primarily because:  
A) Engagement includes enthusiasm and passion for work, not just positive feelings.  
B) Job satisfaction always leads to high levels of employee engagement.  
C) Engagement reflects a deep connection to the company beyond task performance.  
D) Disengaged employees are those who put in time but lack energy or attention.  

#### 8. Which of the following best explains why job attitudes such as job satisfaction, job involvement, and organizational commitment often overlap?  
A) They are all influenced by the employee’s personality traits.  
B) They measure completely different aspects of work experience with no relation.  
C) Employees’ perceptions of pay and promotion opportunities affect only job satisfaction.  
D) Organizational citizenship behaviour is unrelated to these job attitudes.

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following statements accurately describe the dispositional approach to attitude formation?  
A) ✓ Attitudes are stable predispositions similar to personality traits.  
B) ✗ Attitudes change significantly after a person changes jobs (dispositional approach argues attitudes change little).  
C) ✓ Some individuals tend to be happy or unhappy on the job regardless of work conditions.  
D) ✗ Attitudes are primarily shaped by social context and interactions (this is social-information-processing approach).  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 2. The three main components of an attitude include:  
A) ✓ Cognition, Affect, and Behaviour (these are the three components described).  
B) ✗ Beliefs, Values, and Perception (related concepts but not attitude components).  
C) ✗ Emotion, Intention, and Action (similar but not the exact components).  
D) ✗ Motivation, Personality, and Perception (not components of attitude).  

**Correct:** A


#### 3. Cognitive dissonance occurs when:  
A) ✗ An individual’s behaviour is consistent with their attitudes (no dissonance if consistent).  
B) ✓ There is incompatibility between two or more attitudes or between behaviour and attitudes.  
C) ✗ Employees feel satisfied with their job outcomes exceeding expectations (this relates to satisfaction, not dissonance).  
D) ✓ People experience tension due to inconsistency between their actions and beliefs.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 4. Which of the following job dimensions is LEAST likely to directly influence job satisfaction?  
A) ✗ The technical and managerial abilities of supervisors (a key dimension).  
B) ✗ The availability of realistic promotional opportunities (important dimension).  
C) ✓ The employee’s ethnic origin and early family experiences (individual differences, not a job dimension).  
D) ✗ The extent to which coworkers are friendly and supportive (important dimension).  

**Correct:** C


#### 5. Organizational commitment is best characterized by:  
A) ✗ Passive loyalty to the company without emotional attachment (commitment goes beyond passive loyalty).  
B) ✓ Strong belief in organizational goals and values.  
C) ✓ Willingness to exert effort on behalf of the organization.  
D) ✓ Desire to maintain membership in the organization.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 6. Which of the following statements about perceived organizational support (POS) is TRUE?  
A) ✗ POS is unrelated to employee tardiness or customer service quality (POS is linked to lower tardiness and better service).  
B) ✓ Employees with high POS perceptions tend to exhibit more citizenship behaviours.  
C) ✗ POS depends solely on the fairness of pay and rewards (also influenced by voice in decisions and supervisor support).  
D) ✓ Supervisors’ supportiveness influences employees’ perceptions of organizational support.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 7. Employee engagement differs from job satisfaction primarily because:  
A) ✓ Engagement includes enthusiasm and passion for work, not just positive feelings.  
B) ✗ Job satisfaction always leads to high levels of employee engagement (not necessarily true; they are related but distinct).  
C) ✓ Engagement reflects a deep connection to the company beyond task performance.  
D) ✓ Disengaged employees are those who put in time but lack energy or attention.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 8. Which of the following best explains why job attitudes such as job satisfaction, job involvement, and organizational commitment often overlap?  
A) ✓ They are all influenced by the employee’s personality traits.  
B) ✗ They measure completely different aspects of work experience with no relation (they overlap significantly).  
C) ✗ Employees’ perceptions of pay and promotion opportunities affect only job satisfaction (these can influence multiple attitudes).  
D) ✗ Organizational citizenship behaviour is unrelated to these job attitudes (it is related and influenced by attitudes).  

**Correct:** A
