## 3 Personality

## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following statements accurately describe the determinants of personality?  
A) Personality is entirely fixed at conception and does not change throughout life.  
B) Hereditary factors such as genes influence physical and psychological traits that contribute to personality.  
C) Personality traits become more stable as a person moves from adolescence to adulthood.  
D) Environmental factors have no role in shaping personality traits.

#### 2. Regarding the Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI), which of the following are true?  
A) MBTI assumes personality is static and does not account for personality development over time.  
B) MBTI categorizes personality based on four dichotomous pairs, including Thinking vs. Feeling.  
C) MBTI is universally accepted by psychologists as the most valid personality assessment tool.  
D) MBTI includes dimensions such as Extraversion vs. Introversion and Judging vs. Perceiving.

#### 3. Which of the following Big Five personality traits are correctly matched with their descriptions?  
A) Agreeableness – tendency to be cooperative, warm, and trusting.  
B) Conscientiousness – associated with being disorganized and unreliable.  
C) Emotional Stability – ability to withstand stress and remain calm.  
D) Openness to Experience – preference for routine and familiarity over novelty.

#### 4. The Dark Triad traits include Machiavellianism, Narcissism, and Psychopathy. Which of the following statements about these traits are correct?  
A) High Machiavellian individuals tend to manipulate others and may engage in counterproductive work behaviors.  
B) Narcissists generally lack charisma and struggle to succeed in leadership roles.  
C) Psychopaths in organizations often use instrumental bullying to gain power and influence.  
D) Psychopathy is characterized by empathy and remorse for harming others.

#### 5. According to Situation Strength theory, which of the following are true about the influence of personality on behavior?  
A) Personality traits predict behavior more strongly in weak situations than in strong situations.  
B) Strong situations provide clear behavioral cues and discourage inappropriate behavior.  
C) Organizations always benefit from creating strong situations to control employee behavior.  
D) Strong situations may suppress creativity and initiative in employees.

#### 6. Trait Activation Theory (TAT) suggests that:  
A) All personality traits are equally activated in every situation.  
B) Certain situations or events can trigger the expression of specific personality traits.  
C) A commission-based pay plan is likely to activate extraversion more than openness to experience.  
D) Supportive coworker environments reduce the importance of individual prosocial tendencies.

#### 7. Which of the following statements about values and their role in the workplace are correct?  
A) Terminal values refer to desirable end states or goals a person wants to achieve.  
B) Instrumental values are the preferred modes of behavior used to achieve terminal values.  
C) Values have no impact on decision-making or behavior at work.  
D) A balance between terminal and instrumental values is important for effective functioning.

#### 8. Regarding Person-Job Fit and Person-Organization Fit theories, which of the following are accurate?  
A) Person-Job Fit theory suggests satisfaction is highest when personality and job type are congruent.  
B) Person-Organization Fit theory emphasizes matching individual values with organizational culture.  
C) A realistic personality type in a social job is considered a highly congruent fit.  
D) Organizations should prioritize hiring employees whose personalities and values align with the company to reduce turnover.

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following statements accurately describe the determinants of personality?  
A) ✗ Personality is not entirely fixed; it can change, especially during adolescence.  
B) ✓ Hereditary factors like genes influence physical and psychological traits that shape personality.  
C) ✓ Personality traits tend to become more stable from adolescence to adulthood.  
D) ✗ Environmental factors do influence personality, though this question focuses on hereditary determinants.

**Correct:** B, C


#### 2. Regarding the Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI), which of the following are true?  
A) ✓ MBTI assumes personality is static and does not consider development over time.  
B) ✓ MBTI categorizes personality using four dichotomous pairs, including Thinking vs. Feeling.  
C) ✗ MBTI is widely used but criticized by psychologists for lack of scientific validity.  
D) ✓ MBTI includes dimensions such as Extraversion vs. Introversion and Judging vs. Perceiving.

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 3. Which of the following Big Five personality traits are correctly matched with their descriptions?  
A) ✓ Agreeableness refers to being cooperative, warm, and trusting.  
B) ✗ Conscientiousness is associated with being responsible and organized, not disorganized.  
C) ✓ Emotional Stability is the ability to withstand stress and remain calm.  
D) ✗ Openness to Experience involves curiosity and creativity, not preference for routine.

**Correct:** A, C


#### 4. The Dark Triad traits include Machiavellianism, Narcissism, and Psychopathy. Which of the following statements about these traits are correct?  
A) ✓ High Machiavellian individuals manipulate others and may engage in counterproductive behaviors.  
B) ✗ Narcissists tend to be charismatic and often succeed in leadership roles.  
C) ✓ Psychopaths use instrumental bullying to gain power in organizations.  
D) ✗ Psychopathy is characterized by lack of empathy and remorse, not empathy.

**Correct:** A, C


#### 5. According to Situation Strength theory, which of the following are true about the influence of personality on behavior?  
A) ✓ Personality traits predict behavior more strongly in weak situations.  
B) ✓ Strong situations provide clear behavioral cues and discourage inappropriate behavior.  
C) ✗ Organizations do not always benefit from strong situations; they can demotivate and suppress creativity.  
D) ✓ Strong situations may suppress creativity and initiative.

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 6. Trait Activation Theory (TAT) suggests that:  
A) ✗ Not all traits are equally activated in every situation; activation depends on context.  
B) ✓ Certain situations or events trigger the expression of specific personality traits.  
C) ✓ Commission-based pay likely activates extraversion more than openness to experience.  
D) ✓ Supportive coworker environments reduce the impact of individual prosocial tendencies.

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 7. Which of the following statements about values and their role in the workplace are correct?  
A) ✓ Terminal values are desirable end states or goals a person wants to achieve.  
B) ✓ Instrumental values are preferred behaviors used to achieve terminal values.  
C) ✗ Values do influence decision-making and behavior at work.  
D) ✓ Balancing terminal and instrumental values is important for effective functioning.

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 8. Regarding Person-Job Fit and Person-Organization Fit theories, which of the following are accurate?  
A) ✓ Person-Job Fit theory states satisfaction is highest when personality and job type align.  
B) ✓ Person-Organization Fit theory emphasizes matching individual values with organizational culture.  
C) ✗ A realistic personality in a social job is highly incongruent, not congruent.  
D) ✓ Hiring employees whose personalities and values align with the organization reduces turnover.

**Correct:** A, B, D
