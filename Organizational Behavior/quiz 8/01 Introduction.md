## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following statements accurately describe the functions of work?  
A) Work can offer individuals a sense of competence and mastery over their environment.  
B) Work provides social status and can contribute to an individual's identity and self-esteem.  
C) Work primarily serves an economic function by producing goods or services.  
D) Work always guarantees self-actualization for every individual.  

#### 2. Alienation in the workplace is most likely to occur when:  
A) Individuals have opportunities to demonstrate competence and mastery.  
B) Employees feel their work lacks meaning and value to others.  
C) Workers experience powerlessness and normlessness.  
D) Employees receive high monetary compensation but low social recognition.  

#### 3. Organizational Behaviour (OB) studies which three primary determinants of behaviour within organizations?  
A) Economic incentives, social status, and work design  
B) Individuals, groups, and organizational structure  
C) Leadership styles, motivation, and communication  
D) Culture, politics, and technology  

#### 4. The multidisciplinary nature of OB includes contributions from several fields. Which of the following correctly pairs a discipline with its primary focus in OB?  
A) Political Science – analyzing power dynamics within organizational settings  
B) Psychology – examining rites and rituals that influence group behavior  
C) Sociology – understanding how society shapes individual actions within organizations  
D) Anthropology – studying the role of power and politics in organizations  

#### 5. The Hawthorne Studies revealed which of the following insights about worker productivity?  
A) Worker productivity increased partly due to feelings of group pride and importance.  
B) The Hawthorne effect describes how workers’ behavior changed because of researcher involvement.  
C) Monetary incentives were the sole cause of increased productivity.  
D) Positive treatment by managers improved employee output.  

#### 6. Which of the following best illustrates the concept of the "Organizational Iceberg"?  
A) The visible formal structure of an organization is the most important factor in productivity.  
B) Organizations are tangible entities that can be directly observed and measured.  
C) The covert, behavioral aspects of an organization, such as employee attitudes and informal interactions, are often hidden but crucial.  
D) The formal rules and policies of an organization fully explain employee behavior.  

#### 7. Absolutes in Organizational Behaviour imply that:  
A) Generalizations in OB are always applicable regardless of context.  
B) The relationship between cause and effect in OB is often conditional on specific factors.  
C) OB concepts must consider situational or contingency variables to explain behavior accurately.  
D) Human behavior in organizations follows universal laws similar to physical sciences.  

#### 8. Effective management as an integrating activity requires:  
A) Ownership of employees as organizational resources.  
B) Coordinating and guiding individual efforts toward organizational goals while understanding human personality and work systems.  
C) Using scientifically validated information and situational variables to improve interpersonal and leadership skills.  
D) Ignoring individual perceptions and attitudes to focus solely on organizational objectives.

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following statements accurately describe the functions of work?  
A) ✓ Work offers individuals a sense of competence and mastery over their environment.  
B) ✓ Work provides social status and can contribute to identity and self-esteem.  
C) ✓ Work serves an economic function by producing goods or services.  
D) ✗ Work does not always guarantee self-actualization; it is only a possibility for some.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 2. Alienation in the workplace is most likely to occur when:  
A) ✗ Demonstrating competence reduces alienation rather than causing it.  
B) ✓ Lack of meaning and value in work leads to alienation.  
C) ✓ Feelings of powerlessness and normlessness are key signs of alienation.  
D) ✗ High pay alone does not prevent alienation if social recognition is low.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 3. Organizational Behaviour (OB) studies which three primary determinants of behaviour within organizations?  
A) ✗ Economic incentives and social status are functions of work, not the core determinants of OB behavior.  
B) ✓ Individuals, groups, and organizational structure are the core determinants studied in OB.  
C) ✗ Leadership, motivation, and communication are topics but not the three primary determinants.  
D) ✗ Culture, politics, and technology are relevant but not the three main determinants OB focuses on.  

**Correct:** B


#### 4. The multidisciplinary nature of OB includes contributions from several fields. Which of the following correctly pairs a discipline with its primary focus in OB?  
A) ✓ Political Science examines power dynamics within organizations.  
B) ✗ Psychology focuses on human behavior and the mind, not rites and rituals (that’s anthropology).  
C) ✓ Sociology studies how society shapes individual actions within organizations.  
D) ✗ Anthropology studies rites and rituals, not power and politics.  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 5. The Hawthorne Studies revealed which of the following insights about worker productivity?  
A) ✓ Productivity increased partly due to group pride and feeling important.  
B) ✓ The Hawthorne effect describes behavior changes due to researcher involvement.  
C) ✗ Money was not the sole cause of increased productivity according to original findings.  
D) ✓ Positive treatment by managers improved employee output (human relations insight).  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 6. Which of the following best illustrates the concept of the "Organizational Iceberg"?  
A) ✗ The visible formal structure is only the tip and not the most important factor alone.  
B) ✗ Organizations are intangible and cannot be directly observed as a whole.  
C) ✓ The hidden behavioral aspects are crucial and often overlooked.  
D) ✗ Formal rules alone do not fully explain employee behavior.  

**Correct:** C


#### 7. Absolutes in Organizational Behaviour imply that:  
A) ✗ Generalizations are not always applicable regardless of context.  
B) ✓ Cause-effect relationships in OB depend on specific conditions.  
C) ✓ OB concepts must consider situational or contingency variables.  
D) ✗ Human behavior does not follow universal laws like physical sciences.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 8. Effective management as an integrating activity requires:  
A) ✗ People are not owned by the organization; they bring their own perceptions.  
B) ✓ Coordinating efforts toward goals while understanding personality and work systems is essential.  
C) ✓ Using validated information and situational variables improves interpersonal and leadership skills.  
D) ✗ Ignoring individual perceptions harms integration and management effectiveness.  

**Correct:** B, C
