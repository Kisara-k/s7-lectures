## 14 Network Design

## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following are important considerations when aligning network design with business needs?  
A) Competitive advantage  
B) Cost of network infrastructure vs. added value  
C) Return on investment  
D) Number of network protocols supported  

#### 2. When planning for future growth in network design, which factors should be taken into account?  
A) Physical security of network devices only  
B) Expected expansion of applications, services, and servers  
C) Adaptation of new technologies  
D) Use of third-party/public providers for island-wide coverage  

#### 3. Regarding existing infrastructure in network design, which statements are true?  
A) Multi-mode fiber optic backbones are best placed in a central location for star topology  
B) Capital expenditure considerations may justify reusing existing infrastructure  
C) All existing equipment should be discarded to avoid legacy issues  
D) Locations of buildings and physical accessibility influence network design  

#### 4. What are key factors to consider when deciding Internet and intranet requirements for branches?  
A) Balancing controllability versus freedom and confidentiality versus openness  
B) Whether branches should have separate Internet connections or route through head-office  
C) Ensuring all branches have identical bandwidth allocations regardless of usage  
D) Dependency on a single Internet provider and its impact on reliability  

#### 5. In balancing security versus accessibility requirements, which policies and considerations are essential?  
A) Allowing unrestricted access to all servers for ease of use  
B) Organizational and IT security policies  
C) Accessibility for mobile employees  
D) Single sign-on versus distributed authentication  

#### 6. When selecting an appropriate network architecture, which principles should guide the design?  
A) Assume equipment will remain current and relevant for at least ten years  
B) Focus only on server workloads, ignoring traffic loads and application types  
C) Avoid overly complex architectures and estimate current needs accurately  
D) Structured cabling facilitates easier management and cost-effective enhancements  

#### 7. Which design strategies are valid approaches to network design?  
A) Color-coded cable design  
B) Topology (technology/protocol) based design  
C) Security based design  
D) Function (application) based design  

#### 8. In function (application) based design, what should be considered?  
A) Ignoring server and storage requirements  
B) Aligning network design with business drivers, IT infrastructure, and disaster recovery  
C) Different types of business applications and data for various organizations  
D) Designing networks solely based on the fastest available technology  

#### 9. Which of the following are critical elements in security based design?  
A) Use of proxies and NAT for limited access and IP depletion mitigation  
B) Critical node protection such as server farms and VLANs  
C) Physical and network-based threats

#### 10. What are some advanced security mechanisms used in network design?  
A) Multi-tier architectures for critical application communications  
B) Firewalls, IDSs, and IDPs  
C) Disabling VLANs to reduce complexity

#### 11. When considering topology (technology/protocol) based design, which factors are relevant?  
A) Ignoring security concerns over WANs  
B) Differences in LAN, WLAN, and WAN technologies and speeds  
C) Budgetary and environmental constraints  
D) Use of VPNs and tunnels for extended networks  

#### 12. Regarding traffic based design, which considerations are important?  
A) Whether applications can tolerate delays  
B) Designing networks without redundant paths to save costs  
C) Load balancing and switching at wire-speed  
D) Queuing and priority traffic management  

#### 13. Which statements about network infrastructure longevity and upgrades are accurate?  
A) Reusing infrastructure at different network levels can be beneficial if restructured  
B) Throwing away existing infrastructure is always justified to avoid legacy issues  
C) Structured cabling can last longer and ease future enhancements  
D) Most network equipment becomes outdated within three years  

#### 14. In security based design, what is the role of access policies?  
A) Imposing limited access through proxies and NAT  
B) Allowing all employees unrestricted access to all network resources  
C) Granting privileged access using VPNs and role-based authentication  
D) Defining policies for office setups, branches, and third-party access  

#### 15. Which of the following are true about Internet and intranet service considerations?  
A) Public services and internal services require different access controls  
B) Branches should always have independent Internet connections regardless of cost or control needs  
C) Dependency on a single provider can affect reliability and availability  
D) Controllability and policing are irrelevant in intranet design

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following are important considerations when aligning network design with business needs?  
A) ✓ Competitive advantage — Network design should support gaining or maintaining competitive advantage.  
B) ✓ Cost of network infrastructure vs. added value — Balancing cost against business value is essential.  
C) ✓ Return on investment — Cost-effectiveness and ROI are key business considerations.  
D) ✗ Number of network protocols supported — This is a technical detail, not a primary business alignment factor.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 2. When planning for future growth in network design, which factors should be taken into account?  
A) ✗ Physical security of network devices only — Important but too narrow; future growth involves more than security.  
B) ✓ Expected expansion of applications, services, and servers — Growth in these areas drives network scaling.  
C) ✓ Adaptation of new technologies — Networks must evolve with technology changes.  
D) ✓ Use of third-party/public providers for island-wide coverage — Outsourcing and coverage expansion are growth considerations.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 3. Regarding existing infrastructure in network design, which statements are true?  
A) ✓ Multi-mode fiber optic backbones are best placed in a central location for star topology — Centralized core switch placement is recommended.  
B) ✓ Capital expenditure considerations may justify reusing existing infrastructure — Cost savings encourage reuse.  
C) ✗ All existing equipment should be discarded to avoid legacy issues — Throwing away everything is not justified.  
D) ✓ Locations of buildings and physical accessibility influence network design — Physical factors affect infrastructure placement.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 4. What are key factors to consider when deciding Internet and intranet requirements for branches?  
A) ✓ Balancing controllability versus freedom and confidentiality versus openness — Important trade-offs in design.  
B) ✓ Whether branches should have separate Internet connections or route through head-office — Affects control and cost.  
C) ✗ Ensuring all branches have identical bandwidth allocations regardless of usage — Not practical or efficient.  
D) ✓ Dependency on a single Internet provider and its impact on reliability — Single points of failure affect availability.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 5. In balancing security versus accessibility requirements, which policies and considerations are essential?  
A) ✗ Allowing unrestricted access to all servers for ease of use — Contradicts security principles.  
B) ✓ Organizational and IT security policies — Define access and security rules.  
C) ✓ Accessibility for mobile employees — Must balance security with mobility needs.  
D) ✓ Single sign-on versus distributed authentication — Important for managing access control.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 6. When selecting an appropriate network architecture, which principles should guide the design?  
A) ✗ Assume equipment will remain current and relevant for at least ten years — Equipment typically becomes outdated faster.  
B) ✗ Focus only on server workloads, ignoring traffic loads and application types — Traffic and applications are critical factors.  
C) ✓ Avoid overly complex architectures and estimate current needs accurately — Practical and cost-effective approach.  
D) ✓ Structured cabling facilitates easier management and cost-effective enhancements — Structured cabling is a long-term asset.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 7. Which design strategies are valid approaches to network design?  
A) ✗ Color-coded cable design — Not a recognized design strategy, just a cabling practice.  
B) ✓ Topology (technology/protocol) based design — Considers physical and protocol aspects.  
C) ✓ Security based design — Addresses protection needs.  
D) ✓ Function (application) based design — Aligns network with business applications.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 8. In function (application) based design, what should be considered?  
A) ✗ Ignoring server and storage requirements — These are essential components of application support.  
B) ✓ Aligning network design with business drivers, IT infrastructure, and disaster recovery — Ensures business-IT alignment.  
C) ✓ Different types of business applications and data for various organizations — Core to this design approach.  
D) ✗ Designing networks solely based on the fastest available technology — Ignores business needs and data types.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 9. Which of the following are critical elements in security based design?  
A) ✓ Use of proxies and NAT for limited access and IP depletion mitigation — Common security and network management tools.  
B) ✓ Critical node protection such as server farms and VLANs — Protects key network segments.  
C) ✓ Physical and network-based threats — Both must be addressed.  
D) ✗ Allowing open access to all network segments to simplify management — Contradicts security principles.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 10. What are some advanced security mechanisms used in network design?  
A) ✓ Multi-tier architectures for critical application communications — Enhances security by isolation.  
B) ✓ Firewalls, IDSs, and IDPs — Essential for threat detection and prevention.  
C) ✗ Disabling VLANs to reduce complexity — VLANs improve security and segmentation, disabling them reduces security.  
D) ✓ Use of SSL and cryptography for application-level security — Protects data in transit.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 11. When considering topology (technology/protocol) based design, which factors are relevant?  
A) ✗ Ignoring security concerns over WANs — Security is critical over WANs.  
B) ✓ Differences in LAN, WLAN, and WAN technologies and speeds — Affects design choices.  
C) ✓ Budgetary and environmental constraints — Real-world constraints impact design.  
D) ✓ Use of VPNs and tunnels for extended networks — Important for secure WAN extensions.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 12. Regarding traffic based design, which considerations are important?  
A) ✓ Whether applications can tolerate delays — Determines acceptable latency.  
B) ✗ Designing networks without redundant paths to save costs — Redundancy is key for reliability.  
C) ✓ Load balancing and switching at wire-speed — Ensures performance and reliability.  
D) ✓ Queuing and priority traffic management — Manages congestion and QoS.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 13. Which statements about network infrastructure longevity and upgrades are accurate?  
A) ✓ Reusing infrastructure at different network levels can be beneficial if restructured — Can save costs and resources.  
B) ✗ Throwing away existing infrastructure is always justified to avoid legacy issues — Not always cost-effective or necessary.  
C) ✓ Structured cabling can last longer and ease future enhancements — Cabling is a long-term asset.  
D) ✓ Most network equipment becomes outdated within three years — Typical technology lifecycle.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 14. In security based design, what is the role of access policies?  
A) ✓ Imposing limited access through proxies and NAT — Controls and restricts access.  
B) ✗ Allowing all employees unrestricted access to all network resources — Violates security principles.  
C) ✓ Granting privileged access using VPNs and role-based authentication — Ensures only authorized users access sensitive resources.  
D) ✓ Defining policies for office setups, branches, and third-party access — Important for consistent security enforcement.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 15. Which of the following are true about Internet and intranet service considerations?  
A) ✓ Public services and internal services require different access controls — Different security and accessibility needs.  
B) ✗ Branches should always have independent Internet connections regardless of cost or control needs — This depends on design goals and trade-offs.  
C) ✓ Dependency on a single provider can affect reliability and availability — Single points of failure are risky.  
D) ✗ Controllability and policing are irrelevant in intranet design — They are important for security and management.  

**Correct:** A, C
