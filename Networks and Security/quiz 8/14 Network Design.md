## 14 Network Design

## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following are important considerations when aligning network design with business needs?  
A) Determining where to place network resources and services  
B) Ensuring the network infrastructure is the most expensive available  
C) Balancing cost of infrastructure against added business value  
D) Providing competitive advantage through network capabilities  

#### 2. When planning for future growth in network design, which factors should be taken into account?  
A) Expected expansion of applications, services, and servers  
B) The likelihood of completely replacing all existing infrastructure  
C) Adoption of new technologies and diversification of business  
D) Use of third-party providers for island-wide coverage  

#### 3. Regarding existing infrastructure in network design, which statements are true?  
A) It is often necessary to discard all existing equipment to avoid legacy issues  
B) Capital expenditure considerations may justify reusing and restructuring existing infrastructure  
C) Physical locations and accessibility of buildings influence network design decisions  
D) Centralized core switch placement is irrelevant if the backbone is star-wired  

#### 4. In deciding Internet and intranet requirements, which factors must be balanced?  
A) Controllability versus freedom of access  
B) Confidentiality versus openness of information  
C) Reliability and dependency on a single Internet provider  
D) The number of mobile devices connected to the network  

#### 5. Which of the following are key elements in security-based network design?  
A) Use of VPNs and role-based access control for privileged users  
B) Ignoring physical threats if network-based threats are addressed  
C) Deployment of firewalls, IDS, and IDP systems to protect critical nodes  
D) Avoiding subnet isolation to reduce firewall interfaces  

#### 6. What are the main considerations in topology (technology/protocol) based network design?  
A) Differences in LAN, WLAN, and WAN technologies and speeds  
B) Budgetary and environmental constraints affecting technology choice  
C) The use of PPTP and L2TP protocols for secure extended networks  
D) Designing networks without regard to security over WANs  

#### 7. Traffic-based network design must address which of the following challenges?  
A) Prioritizing traffic and avoiding congestion to meet application requirements  
B) Ensuring all applications accept delays equally  
C) Implementing load balancing and redundant paths for reliability  
D) Ignoring latency and throughput since they do not affect user experience  

#### 8. When selecting an appropriate network architecture, which principles should guide the design?  
A) Avoid overly complex architectures and estimate current needs realistically  
B) Assume all equipment will remain current and functional for at least ten years  
C) Recognize that structured cabling facilitates easier management and future enhancements  
D) Design solely based on the most advanced technology available, regardless of cost or necessity

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following are important considerations when aligning network design with business needs?  
A) ✓ Determining where to place network resources and services is essential to meet business goals.  
B) ✗ The most expensive infrastructure is not necessarily better; cost-effectiveness matters.  
C) ✓ Balancing cost against added business value ensures investment returns.  
D) ✓ Providing competitive advantage through network capabilities aligns with business strategy.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 2. When planning for future growth in network design, which factors should be taken into account?  
A) ✓ Expansion of applications, services, and servers directly impacts network capacity needs.  
B) ✗ Completely replacing all infrastructure is usually not justified or necessary.  
C) ✓ Adopting new technologies and business diversification affect future network requirements.  
D) ✓ Using third-party providers for wide coverage is a common growth consideration.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 3. Regarding existing infrastructure in network design, which statements are true?  
A) ✗ Discarding all equipment is discouraged; reuse and restructuring are preferred.  
B) ✓ Capital expenditure concerns justify reusing and restructuring existing infrastructure.  
C) ✓ Physical locations and accessibility influence network design decisions.  
D) ✗ Centralized core switch placement is important, especially with star-wired backbones.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 4. In deciding Internet and intranet requirements, which factors must be balanced?  
A) ✓ Controllability versus freedom of access is a key design trade-off.  
B) ✓ Confidentiality versus openness affects security and accessibility policies.  
C) ✓ Reliability and dependency on a single provider are critical considerations.  
D) ✗ Number of mobile devices is relevant but not explicitly highlighted as a balancing factor here.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 5. Which of the following are key elements in security-based network design?  
A) ✓ VPNs and role-based access control protect privileged access.  
B) ✗ Physical threats must also be considered, not ignored.  
C) ✓ Firewalls, IDS, and IDP systems protect critical nodes and network segments.  
D) ✗ Subnet isolation increases security by limiting exposure, so avoiding it is incorrect.  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 6. What are the main considerations in topology (technology/protocol) based network design?  
A) ✓ Differences in LAN, WLAN, and WAN technologies and speeds affect design.  
B) ✓ Budgetary and environmental constraints influence technology choices.  
C) ✓ PPTP and L2TP protocols are relevant for secure extended networks.  
D) ✗ Security over WANs is important and should not be ignored.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 7. Traffic-based network design must address which of the following challenges?  
A) ✓ Prioritizing traffic and congestion avoidance are essential for performance.  
B) ✗ Not all applications accept delays equally; this must be considered.  
C) ✓ Load balancing and redundant paths improve reliability and availability.  
D) ✗ Latency and throughput significantly affect user experience and must be managed.  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 8. When selecting an appropriate network architecture, which principles should guide the design?  
A) ✓ Avoiding unnecessary complexity and estimating current needs is practical.  
B) ✗ Equipment typically becomes outdated within a few years, not ten.  
C) ✓ Structured cabling supports easier management and cost-effective upgrades.  
D) ✗ Designing solely on advanced technology without regard to cost or necessity is unwise.  

**Correct:** A, C
