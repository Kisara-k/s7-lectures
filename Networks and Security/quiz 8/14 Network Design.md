## 14 Network Design

## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following are important considerations when aligning network design with business needs?  
A) Providing competitive advantage through network capabilities  
B) Determining where to place network resources and services  
C) Balancing cost of infrastructure against added business value  
D) Ensuring the network infrastructure is the most expensive available  

#### 2. When planning for future growth in network design, which factors should be taken into account?  
A) Use of third-party providers for island-wide coverage  
B) Adoption of new technologies and diversification of business  
C) Expected expansion of applications, services, and servers  
D) The likelihood of completely replacing all existing infrastructure  

#### 3. Regarding existing infrastructure in network design, which statements are true?  
A) It is often necessary to discard all existing equipment to avoid legacy issues  
B) Capital expenditure considerations may justify reusing and restructuring existing infrastructure  
C) Centralized core switch placement is irrelevant if the backbone is star-wired  
D) Physical locations and accessibility of buildings influence network design decisions  

#### 4. In deciding Internet and intranet requirements, which factors must be balanced?  
A) Confidentiality versus openness of information  
B) The number of mobile devices connected to the network  
C) Controllability versus freedom of access  
D) Reliability and dependency on a single Internet provider  

#### 5. Which of the following are key elements in security-based network design?  
A) Ignoring physical threats if network-based threats are addressed  
B) Avoiding subnet isolation to reduce firewall interfaces  
C) Deployment of firewalls, IDS, and IDP systems to protect critical nodes  
D) Use of VPNs and role-based access control for privileged users  

#### 6. What are the main considerations in topology (technology/protocol) based network design?  
A) Designing networks without regard to security over WANs  
B) The use of PPTP and L2TP protocols for secure extended networks  
C) Budgetary and environmental constraints affecting technology choice  
D) Differences in LAN, WLAN, and WAN technologies and speeds  

#### 7. Traffic-based network design must address which of the following challenges?  
A) Prioritizing traffic and avoiding congestion to meet application requirements  
B) Ignoring latency and throughput since they do not affect user experience  
C) Ensuring all applications accept delays equally  
D) Implementing load balancing and redundant paths for reliability  

#### 8. When selecting an appropriate network architecture, which principles should guide the design?  
A) Assume all equipment will remain current and functional for at least ten years  
B) Avoid overly complex architectures and estimate current needs realistically  
C) Recognize that structured cabling facilitates easier management and future enhancements  
D) Design solely based on the most advanced technology available, regardless of cost or necessity

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following are important considerations when aligning network design with business needs?  
A) ✓ Providing competitive advantage through network capabilities aligns with business strategy.  
B) ✓ Determining where to place network resources and services is essential to meet business goals.  
C) ✓ Balancing cost against added business value ensures investment returns.  
D) ✗ The most expensive infrastructure is not necessarily better; cost-effectiveness matters.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 2. When planning for future growth in network design, which factors should be taken into account?  
A) ✓ Using third-party providers for wide coverage is a common growth consideration.  
B) ✓ Adopting new technologies and business diversification affect future network requirements.  
C) ✓ Expansion of applications, services, and servers directly impacts network capacity needs.  
D) ✗ Completely replacing all infrastructure is usually not justified or necessary.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 3. Regarding existing infrastructure in network design, which statements are true?  
A) ✗ Discarding all equipment is discouraged; reuse and restructuring are preferred.  
B) ✓ Capital expenditure concerns justify reusing and restructuring existing infrastructure.  
C) ✗ Centralized core switch placement is important, especially with star-wired backbones.  
D) ✓ Physical locations and accessibility influence network design decisions.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 4. In deciding Internet and intranet requirements, which factors must be balanced?  
A) ✓ Confidentiality versus openness affects security and accessibility policies.  
B) ✗ Number of mobile devices is relevant but not explicitly highlighted as a balancing factor here.  
C) ✓ Controllability versus freedom of access is a key design trade-off.  
D) ✓ Reliability and dependency on a single provider are critical considerations.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 5. Which of the following are key elements in security-based network design?  
A) ✗ Physical threats must also be considered, not ignored.  
B) ✗ Subnet isolation increases security by limiting exposure, so avoiding it is incorrect.  
C) ✓ Firewalls, IDS, and IDP systems protect critical nodes and network segments.  
D) ✓ VPNs and role-based access control protect privileged access.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 6. What are the main considerations in topology (technology/protocol) based network design?  
A) ✗ Security over WANs is important and should not be ignored.  
B) ✓ PPTP and L2TP protocols are relevant for secure extended networks.  
C) ✓ Budgetary and environmental constraints influence technology choices.  
D) ✓ Differences in LAN, WLAN, and WAN technologies and speeds affect design.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 7. Traffic-based network design must address which of the following challenges?  
A) ✓ Prioritizing traffic and congestion avoidance are essential for performance.  
B) ✗ Latency and throughput significantly affect user experience and must be managed.  
C) ✗ Not all applications accept delays equally; this must be considered.  
D) ✓ Load balancing and redundant paths improve reliability and availability.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 8. When selecting an appropriate network architecture, which principles should guide the design?  
A) ✗ Equipment typically becomes outdated within a few years, not ten.  
B) ✓ Avoiding unnecessary complexity and estimating current needs is practical.  
C) ✓ Structured cabling supports easier management and cost-effective upgrades.  
D) ✗ Designing solely on advanced technology without regard to cost or necessity is unwise.  

**Correct:** B, C
